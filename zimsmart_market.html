How can this shop make money?

1. Start with free listings. Ask real sellers for permission to list their products, then help connect buyers and sellers.

2. Earn a disclosed referral fee. Agree with a seller in writing on a small commission for completed sales. Do not promise sales.

3. Add advertising later. An ad network may require an eligible site/app, original content, privacy disclosures, policy compliance and sufficient review/approval. Ad income is not guaranteed and is usually tiny without visitors.

4. Promote responsibly. Share the link in WhatsApp Status, Facebook groups where promotion is allowed, and TikTok. Don't spam or post fake products or reviews.
